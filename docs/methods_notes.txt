**METHODS NOTES**

**Scope and selection**
* We analyze contracts that begin between 1700 and 1780 inclusive because coverage is near complete in this period.
* Earlier decades are fragmentary, and the final decades are less consistent due to chartered ships and foreign regiments.
* Rows dropped due to missing/invalid start dates when filtering: **159,657**.

**Origins and regions**
* We use the dataset’s nine region codes (A-I) to make analysis feasible and comparable with prior work.
* We acknowledge possible misclassification from standardization and loss of local variation.
* **99.5%** of contracts contain a raw origin string; **82.7%** could be linked to a standardized region via the bridge.
* “Unknown” regions reflect missing/ambiguous IDs in the bridge, not the absence of origin information.

**Ranks**
* Detailed ranks are mapped to six parent categories and supplemented with a numeric ladder that approximates seniority.
* Rank seniority is derived from median wage quintiles in _voc_ranks.csv_; ranks in the top two quintiles are classified as “high rank.”

**Outcomes**
* Raw reasons are grouped into four analytical outcome categories: _Death, Repatriated, Attrition_, and _Unknown_.

**Dropped or missing**
* Rows before filtering (as documented by the dataset): **774,200**.
* Rows after filtering to 1700-1780: **614,543**.
* Rows dropped when building the modeling dataset: **to be added after 03_models.py**.

**Reproducibility**
* Cleaning is scripted in _01_cleaning.py_. Descriptives are in _02_descriptives.py_. Modeling is in _03_models.py_.
* Outputs are saved in _data_clean_, _tables_, and _figures_.
* This file is automatically overwritten each run.
