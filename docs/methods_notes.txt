METHODS NOTES

Scope and selection
- We analyze contracts that begin between 1700 and 1780 inclusive because coverage is near complete in this period.
- Earlier decades are fragmentary, and the final decades are less consistent due to chartered ships and foreign regiments.

Origins and regions
- We use the dataset’s nine region codes (A to I) to make analysis feasible and comparable with prior work.
- We acknowledge possible misclassification from standardization and loss of local variation.
- Percent of region == "0" (Unknown) after merging: TO_FILL_PERCENT

Ranks
- Detailed ranks are mapped to six parent categories and ordered into a numeric ladder that approximates seniority.
- Unmapped ranks are reviewed and incorporated before final outputs.

Outcomes
- Raw reasons are grouped into four analytical outcome categories.
- Ambiguous categories are flagged and can be handled in sensitivity checks.

Dropped or missing
- Rows dropped due to missing start dates: TO_FILL_N
- Rows dropped when building modeling dataset: TO_FILL_N

Reproducibility
- Cleaning and analysis are scripted in notebooks/01_cleaning.py.
- All outputs are saved in data_clean, tables, and figures.
