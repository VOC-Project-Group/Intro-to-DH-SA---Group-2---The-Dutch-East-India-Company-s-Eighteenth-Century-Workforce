METHODS NOTES

Scope and selection
- Filtered contracts to 1700–1780: 774,200 → 676,934

Origins and regions
- Raw origin coverage: 99.5%
- Region coverage after fallbacks: 81.6%

Ranks
- Ranks mapped to 6 parent categories; seniority derived from wage quintiles.

Outcomes
- Outcomes grouped into: Death / Repatriated / Attrition / Unknown.
